GREEN GRIN GOOGLE SHEETS FEED

1. In Netlify, add GREEN_GRIN_SHEETS_SYNC_KEY with a long private value you make up.
2. Redeploy the portal site.
3. Create or open your Google Sheet.
4. In the Sheet, open Extensions -> Apps Script.
5. Replace the sample code with Code.gs from this folder and click Save.
6. In Apps Script, open Project Settings -> Script properties.
7. Add GREEN_GRIN_SITE_URL with your portal site address, such as https://portal.greengrinlawns.com
8. Add GREEN_GRIN_SHEETS_SYNC_KEY with the exact same private value used in Netlify.
9. Return to the Sheet and reload it.
10. Open Green Grin -> Install daily refresh and approve Google's permissions.

The Customers tab will be created and refreshed once per day around 6:00 AM in the Apps Script project's time zone. Do not share the sync key or commit it to GitHub.
